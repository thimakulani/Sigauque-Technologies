Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how QuickPOS (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use the QuickPOS store management app or OrderSync customer ordering app (together, the “Services”). It is intended to describe the Services as currently provided. Some features depend on the app and options you use.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, email address, phone number, password credentials, and store or profile details you provide when you register or use an account.
  • Orders and business records: products, inventory, cart and order details, delivery instructions or address, customer details entered for an order, payment status and transaction references, and related support communications.
  • Location: if you choose the location feature, the app may access your device location while you use it to help select or provide a delivery location. You can deny permission and enter an address manually. Store addresses you enter are also processed to provide store features.
  • Camera and images: the store app may use the camera when you choose barcode scanning. Images you choose to upload, such as product images, may be processed to provide that feature.
  • Device and service information: a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token used for store notifications, and technical information such as IP address and request or activity records generated when you use the Services.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; operate store, inventory, ordering, delivery and payment features; send order, account and requested store notifications; respond to support requests; maintain, troubleshoot and protect the Services; and meet legal obligations.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We share only what is needed to operate the feature you use, including with:

  • Stores and customers: order and delivery details are shared between the customer and the store fulfilling the order.
  • Service providers: hosting and database providers, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, Google Maps services for map and location features, and Cloudinary for images.
  • Payment providers: Paystack, Stripe, or other payment providers enabled for a transaction receive information needed to process or reconcile that payment. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by QuickPOS.
  • Legal and safety purposes: information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Services, or the public.

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy terms. Their processing may take place in countries other than your own.

Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

You can manage device permissions in your device settings, including location, camera, and notifications. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact us using the details below. We may retain information where needed for legal, security, or legitimate transaction-record purposes and will explain any applicable limits when responding.

Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and people placing or managing orders, and are not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change. The current version and its effective date will be available at this page.
