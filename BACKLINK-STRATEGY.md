# Sigauque Technologies backlink strategy

## Goal

Earn relevant, editorial links that help South African schools, retailers, and
businesses discover the company's software and development services. Prioritise
trust and referral value over link volume.

## Priority opportunities

1. **Founder and company profiles:** Complete the founder's LinkedIn profile and
   company page with a link to `https://sigauque-technology.co.za/`. Keep the
   business name, Johannesburg location, services, and contact details
   consistent.
2. **Product partners:** Ask the School Connect and QuickPOS collaborators,
   pilot customers, and integration partners to include a short attribution or
   case-study link where it is genuinely useful to their visitors.
3. **Customer stories:** Publish one concise case study per completed project,
   with the customer's approval, measurable outcomes, and links from the
   customer's own news or partner page when appropriate.
4. **South African business directories:** Submit accurate company details to
   established local business and technology directories. Reject directories
   that sell bulk links, require keyword-stuffed descriptions, or have no real
   audience.
5. **Useful expert contributions:** Offer practical articles or interviews to
   South African education, retail, and small-business publications. Each pitch
   should answer a specific audience question and link to a relevant page only
   where the editor considers it helpful.
6. **Community and events:** Participate in local technology, school, and
   business associations. Seek links from event speaker pages, workshop
   resources, or member profiles when participation warrants them.

## Monthly routine

- Contact three well-matched organisations with a specific, personal proposal.
- Publish or improve one useful resource that can earn citations, such as a
  school-software selection checklist or a point-of-sale implementation guide.
- Record referring page, target page, relationship, date contacted, outcome,
  and referral visits in a simple spreadsheet.
- Review new links monthly; request correction of inaccurate company details
  and disavow nothing automatically.

## Guardrails

Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, use private blog networks, automate
comment links, or promise keyword-rich anchor text. Ask for natural company or
page-name references. A link should make sense to the referring site's readers
even if it had no search-ranking effect.
